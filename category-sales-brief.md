## PHASE 1: CATEGORY DESIGN (from 3 questions)

### The 3 Questions + Answers (v4 - Your Art Not Their Template, 2026-11-16)

1. **"What does everyone in your market believe that you think is wrong?"**
   Your sessions are a template handed to you by your teacher training. Your teacher gave you something incredible - the foundation. But your essence and art are what make it your own.

2. **"Who has tried to solve this and found nothing works? What did they try?"**
   Facilitators trying to do what their teacher taught them but "better" - better songs, better intros, better guidances. But if you're running the same format with the same tools, you'll always sound like a version of them. The breakthrough: you do something different. You learned that the safer people feel, the deeper they drop. And the deeper they drop, the larger the shift. So you add something your teacher never had. You give participants headsets. Now your sessions have evolved beyond the format you were taught.

3. **"What would you say about the old way that makes incumbents uncomfortable?"**
   People feel safer with headphones. They drop in deeper. Because they drop in deeper, you create bigger shifts.

### The "Too Expensive" Reframe

The facilitator compares: $780 headphones vs $0 speakers. That math only works if they're in the same category. They're not.

**The real ROI math:**
- Facilitator charges $30-80/person, 20 participants = $600-1,600/session
- If headphones make shifts better, participants return more AND refer others
- ONE extra returning participant/session = $30-80 x 50 sessions/year = $1,500-4,000/year
- Headphones cost $780 and last 2+ years
- **$780 pays for itself if headphones bring back ONE extra person per session**

### Grand Slam Offer: The Guided Wellness Audio Kit

**Core offer (DWY):**
- 20 guided wellness headphones + 1 transmitter + charging station
- **Session Transition Guide** — how to introduce headphones to a group used to speakers (the script, the framing, the first 5 minutes)
- **Wellness Playlist Pack** — 10 curated playlists by session type, mixed for headphone intimacy (breathwork 4-7-8 timing, ecstatic dance BPM arcs, sound journey frequencies)
- **"Your First Headphone Session" call** (30 min with Cisco) — plan the transition together

**Bonuses (exceed core value):**

- Bonus 2: **Charging & Logistics Cheat Sheet** — how to charge 20-30 headsets, transport, hand out, collect ($100 value)
- Bonus 3: **"The Safety Script"** — exact words to frame headphones as safety tool ("these create a cocoon of sound so you can drop in without hearing the person next to you") ($200 value)
- Bonus 4: **Private facilitator community** — other facilitators running headphone sessions, sharing what works ($200/year value)

**Guarantee (conditional, activation point = 3 sessions):**
"Run 3 headphone sessions. If your participants don't report deeper shifts than your speaker sessions, we'll review your setup and either fix it or take the headphones back. You keep the playlists and guides."

3 sessions (not 1) because first session has a learning curve. Costs nothing because the product works.
