## Day 2: Business Foundations

**Lessons:** Customer First Brand Formula, Setting Up Your Business

**Key Takeaways:**
- A "Customer First Brand" uses dropshipping fulfillment but focuses on problem-solving products, branded stores, fast shipping, and great customer service -- the opposite of traditional dropshipping
- You connect customer to product and collect profit in between without holding stock
- Register as a sole trader (AU), sole proprietor (US), or limited company (UK)
- Separate business and personal finances from day one

**Action Steps:**

## Day 28: Data Analysis & Optimization

**Lessons:** Full Store Analysis, Optimising Your Campaign

**Key Takeaways:**
- Track data in the Notion dashboard every 2 days: Facebook metrics (spend, CPM, CTR, CPC) + Shopify/Triple Whale metrics (sessions, add-to-carts, purchases, revenue, net profit, net margin)
- Key benchmarks: CPM < $20, CTR > 1%, CPC < $2, conversion rate > 2%, net margin > 25%
- Breakeven ROAS is the primary profitability indicator
- "Conversion bleeds" = metrics below KPI that are costing you profit
- Kill ad sets that spend past breakeven cost-per-purchase with no sales
- Horizontal audience scaling: turn off losers, duplicate winners with new related interests
- Hook rate benchmark: >30%; average video play time: >3 seconds
- Facebook attribution delays are normal -- check turned-off ad sets for delayed purchases
- Use the "raving customer method" to get initial engagement on your ads from the Freedom community
- Never touch what is working -- leave profitable ad sets untouched
- Within ad sets, turn off the underperforming ad creative to concentrate spend on the winner

**Action Steps:**
1. After 2 full days of ads, enter all data into the Notion tracker
2. Compare each metric against benchmarks to identify conversion bleeds
3. Use the "How to Improve Metrics" table to find fixes for each underperforming metric
4. Turn off ad sets that exceeded breakeven cost-per-purchase without sales
5. Duplicate best-performing ad sets with new related interest audiences
6. Check hook rate and video play time per creative; turn off underperforming ads within ad sets
7. Re-check previously turned-off ad sets for delayed purchase attribution
8. Repeat this optimization cycle every 2 days

**Lessons:** Customer Support Walkthrough, What's Next?

**Key Takeaways:**
- Use your brand Gmail as the central customer support hub; unsubscribe from all promotional emails
- Reply to customer emails, then archive -- keep inbox clean as a to-do list
- Manage Facebook/Instagram messages and comments via Meta Business Suite inbox
- Hide spam/scam messages and promotional solicitations; never respond to "page deletion" scams
- Reply to every ad comment to build engagement: react with emoji + ask a follow-up question to keep conversation going
- Ad comment sections become de facto FAQ pages -- answer product questions publicly
- Issue refunds through Shopify: Orders > select order > Refund (full or partial) with notification to customer
- Customer feedback is product intelligence -- track suggestions for new products/improvements

**Action Steps:**
1. Check brand email daily: respond to all customer messages, then archive
2. Check Meta Business Suite inbox daily: respond to genuine DMs, hide/delete spam
3. Reply to every Facebook and Instagram ad comment with a reaction + follow-up question
4. Track any product suggestions or recurring questions from customers
5. Process refunds promptly when needed through Shopify's order refund feature
6. Consider the Freedom Formula program for advanced scaling strategies
