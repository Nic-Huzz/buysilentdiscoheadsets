Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Yoga & Wellness

Can silent disco headphones be used for outdoor yoga?

This is one of our most popular use cases. Silent disco headphones eliminate wind noise, traffic sounds, and other outdoor distractions. Your participants hear your voice and music with perfect clarity, even on a beach or in a park.

Are the headphones comfortable for yoga and meditation?

Yes. Our headphones feature padded ear cups and adjustable headbands designed for extended wear. They're lightweight enough for yoga poses including inversions, and the over-ear design stays secure during movement.

How do breathwork facilitators use silent disco headphones?

The facilitator connects a microphone to the transmitter. Participants hear guided breathing cues, music, and voice guidance directly in their ears, creating an incredibly intimate and immersive experience. Many facilitators say it doubles the depth of their sessions.

Can I run multiple wellness sessions simultaneously?

Yes — that's the power of 3 channels. Run a yoga class on Channel 1, a meditation session on Channel 2, and a dance/movement class on Channel 3, all in the same space at the same time.
