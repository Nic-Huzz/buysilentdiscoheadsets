Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Ecstatic Dance

Does ecstatic dance work with headphones, or does it lose the shared experience?

It works beautifully — everyone hears the same journey at the same moment, so the collective wave stays fully intact. Dancers consistently report going deeper because the music feels closer and the outside world disappears. And the moment everyone removes headphones together for the closing circle is genuinely magic.

Can I host ecstatic dance outdoors or in noise-restricted venues?

That's exactly what silent disco headphones are for. Beaches, parks, rooftops, residential neighbourhoods, venues with strict curfews — none of them are obstacles anymore. There's no external sound, so there's nothing to complain about and no permit issues around amplified music in most locations.

How does the DJ set work with silent disco headphones?

The DJ connects their mixer output to the transmitter via standard 3.5mm or RCA cables and plays exactly as they would through a sound system. The set broadcasts to every headphone in real time with zero delay, up to 500m away.

How many headphones do I need for an ecstatic dance event?

One per dancer — unlike a party, everyone is dancing the whole time. Most community dances run 40-80 people, so 50-100 headphones covers regular events with spares. For hosts running ticketed events regularly, a purchased set typically pays for itself within a handful of dances compared to renting.
