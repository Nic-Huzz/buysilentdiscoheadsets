Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Corporate Events

How do silent disco headphones work for conferences?

Each speaker connects to a different channel on the transmitter. Attendees wear headphones and switch between channels (talks) freely. It's like having 3 breakout rooms in one space — saving venue costs and giving attendees more choice.

Can we brand the headphones for our corporate event?

Yes. We offer custom branding options including logo placement on the headband. For large orders, we can also customise the LED colours to match your brand. Contact us for corporate branding packages.

Do you offer bulk pricing for corporate orders?

Absolutely. We offer volume discounts starting at 50+ units. Corporate packages include headphones, transmitters, charging equipment, and a dedicated account manager. Contact our sales team for a custom quote.

What's the return on investment for buying vs renting?

If you run 3+ events per year, buying is almost always cheaper than renting. Our headphones last 5+ years with proper care. A 100-unit purchase pays for itself after just 2-3 events compared to rental pricing.
