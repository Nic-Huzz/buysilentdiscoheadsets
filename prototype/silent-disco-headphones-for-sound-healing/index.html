Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Sound Healing

Why are headphones better than speakers for sound healing?

Control and accuracy. Room acoustics distort what each client hears — headphones bypass the room entirely, delivering your intended frequencies identically to every client. And for binaural beats specifically, headphones aren't just better, they're required: each ear must receive a separate frequency for the effect to occur.

Do binaural beats and brainwave entrainment work through these headphones?

Yes. The headphones deliver true stereo with full left/right separation, which is exactly what binaural entrainment requires. Play any binaural or isochronic track through the transmitter and every client receives it as the track's creator intended.

Can I combine live instruments with recorded frequencies?

Yes. Run a microphone (for voice or miked instruments) and a playback device through a small mixer into the transmitter. You can speak or play live over recorded frequency beds — a setup many practitioners use for guided healing journeys.

Is headphone volume safe for long sound healing sessions?

Yes. The headphones have onboard volume control and comfortable listening happens at moderate levels — typically lower than speaker setups need, because there's no room noise to overcome. Clients can adjust their own volume to what feels right for their body.
