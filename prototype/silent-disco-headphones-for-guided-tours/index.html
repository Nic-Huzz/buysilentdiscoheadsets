Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Guided Tours

Can I use a microphone with the silent disco transmitter?

Yes. Connect any microphone (lapel mic, handheld, or headset) to the transmitter's audio input. Your voice is broadcast to all headphones on that channel in real-time with zero delay.

What's the range for guided tours?

Our transmitter has a 500m wireless range in open spaces. In buildings or urban areas, expect 100-150m. This is more than enough for any walking tour — your group can spread out comfortably without losing audio.

Can I run tours in multiple languages at once?

Yes — this is a major advantage of the 3-channel system. Have a guide speaking English on Channel 1, French on Channel 2, and Spanish on Channel 3. Participants select their language by switching channels.

How long does the battery last for a full tour day?

Our headphones last 8+ hours on a single charge — enough for a full day of tours. For operators running multiple tours per day, we include rapid-charge solutions that get headphones ready between sessions.
