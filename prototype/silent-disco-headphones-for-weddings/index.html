Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Weddings

How many silent disco headphones do I need for my wedding?

We recommend 1 headphone per 2 guests for a wedding, since not everyone dances at once. For a 100-person wedding, 50-75 headphones is ideal. For a 150+ guest wedding, 75-100 headphones ensures everyone who wants to dance can.

Can I use silent disco headphones for an outdoor wedding?

Absolutely — this is one of the most popular uses. Silent disco is perfect for outdoor weddings, garden parties, and marquee receptions where noise restrictions apply. The headphones have a 500m range from the transmitter, so your entire venue is covered.

What music can we play on the 3 channels?

Anything you want. Each channel connects to a separate audio source — a DJ, laptop, phone, or mixer. Most wedding couples set up Channel 1 for classics and slow dances, Channel 2 for pop and RnB, and Channel 3 for dance music. Your DJ can manage all three.

Do silent disco headphones work with a wedding DJ?

Yes. Your DJ connects their mixer output to the transmitter. They can manage all 3 channels from their setup, making announcements and controlling the music just like a normal reception — but through headphones instead of speakers.
