Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco Fundraising

Do you offer special pricing for charity events?

Yes! We offer discounted rates for registered charities and non-profit fundraisers. Contact us with your charity registration details for special nonprofit pricing. We want to help you maximize funds raised for your cause.

How does silent disco help with fundraising?

Silent disco creates a unique, memorable experience that gets people talking, sharing on social media, and staying engaged longer at your event. Happy, entertained guests are more likely to donate generously. Plus, you can run silent auctions and music simultaneously without noise interference.

Can we run silent auctions during the silent disco?

Absolutely! Channel 1 can be used for auction announcements and updates, while Channels 2 and 3 play background music. Guests can tune in to auction updates when needed, then switch back to music. This keeps the energy high while ensuring important announcements are heard.

How long does setup take for a fundraising event?

Setup takes just 15-30 minutes. We provide full setup instructions, and many fundraising teams handle it themselves. For larger events, we can arrange professional setup service. The key is having your audio sources (DJ, playlist, microphone) ready to connect.
