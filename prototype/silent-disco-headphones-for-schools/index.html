3

5

Create a "silent disco zone" where kids without headphones can still enjoy watching

Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Schools

What age range are silent disco headphones suitable for?

Our headphones are suitable for ages 8 and up. They feature adjustable headbands to fit children's heads comfortably and safely. For younger children (5-7), we recommend shorter sessions with adult supervision.

Do you offer bulk pricing for schools?

Yes! Schools qualify for our educational discount. Contact us for bulk pricing on 30+ headphones. We also offer payment plans for larger orders and can work with school budgets and purchase order systems.

How much supervision do kids need with silent disco headphones?

Minimal supervision is needed once kids understand the basics. Teachers should explain how to change channels, adjust volume, and handle the headphones carefully. We recommend 1 adult supervisor per 15-20 children for the smoothest experience.

Can we use these for educational activities beyond parties?

Absolutely! Many schools use them for language learning (different languages on each channel), meditation sessions, focus time with concentration music, and even quiet study periods where students can choose background music or silence.
