Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Wellness Events

How many headphones does a wellness event or retreat need?

Count your largest single session, not total attendance. If your biggest session hosts 60 people, 60-75 headphones (with spares) covers the whole event, since sessions run sequentially or split across channels. For wellness festivals with parallel programming, plan one headphone per concurrent participant.

Should a retreat centre or event producer buy or rent silent disco headphones?

If you run events regularly, buying wins quickly. At typical rental rates, a purchased fleet pays for itself within a few events — and you gain the freedom to use headphones for every class, not just special occasions. All our equipment comes with a 1-year warranty.

Can different facilitators use the system without technical knowledge?

Yes. The transmitter is plug-and-play: connect a phone, laptop, or microphone via standard 3.5mm or RCA cable and it broadcasts instantly. Facilitators need a 5-minute briefing at most — there's no software, pairing, or configuration.

Do the headphones work for both loud formats (dance) and quiet formats (meditation)?

Yes — that's what makes them ideal for wellness events. Each guest controls their own volume, the sound quality handles everything from bass-heavy dance journeys to whispered meditation guidance, and the LED lights can glow for dance parties or switch off for stillness.
