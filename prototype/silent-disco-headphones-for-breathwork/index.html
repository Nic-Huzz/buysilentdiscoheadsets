Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Breathwork

How do I use a microphone with silent disco headphones for breathwork?

Connect your microphone (lapel, headset, or handheld) and your music source to the transmitter. Both are broadcast together to every headphone in real time with zero delay, so you can guide breathing cues over the music exactly as you would with speakers — but with far more intimacy and clarity.

Why do breathwork facilitators prefer headphones over speakers?

Immersion. With speakers, participants hear the room — echoes, traffic, other people. With headphones, they hear only your voice and the music, which helps them drop into altered states faster and stay there longer. Many facilitators report noticeably deeper sessions after switching.

Can I run breathwork sessions outdoors with silent disco headphones?

Yes — this is one of the most popular use cases. Wind, waves, and background noise disappear entirely. Our transmitter has a 500m open-air range and the headphones have 10+ hours of battery, so beach ceremonies, jungle retreats, and rooftop sessions all work perfectly.

Do binaural beats work through silent disco headphones?

Yes, and this is a key advantage: binaural beats only work through headphones, because each ear must receive a slightly different frequency. Through speakers the effect is lost. Our headphones deliver true stereo separation, so brainwave entrainment tracks work as designed.
