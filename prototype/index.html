What Are Silent Disco Headphones?

Silent disco headphones are wireless headsets that receive audio from an RF transmitter, letting groups listen to music or guided audio without speakers. Each pair features LED lights that glow red, green, or blue to show which of the 3 channels the listener has selected, creating a vibrant, noise-free group experience.
