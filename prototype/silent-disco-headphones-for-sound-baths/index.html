A sound bath is only as good as what your guests can hear. In large venues, outdoor spaces, or rooms with poor acoustics, the delicate overtones of crystal bowls and gongs get lost — the guests nearest the instruments get a completely different experience to those at the back. Silent disco headphones fix this: mic your instruments into the transmitter, and every guest receives the full, detailed sound at identical volume, wherever they're lying. It also unlocks sound baths in places they were never possible before — beaches, parks, rooftops, and noisy urban venues.

Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Disco for Sound Baths

Doesn't using headphones defeat the purpose of a sound bath?

It's a fair question — and the answer is the opposite. Guests still feel the physical vibration of the instruments in the room; the headphones add what most guests were missing: the full harmonic detail. Practitioners tell us guests at the back of the room finally experience what the front row always got. And for outdoor or large-venue events, headphones are the only way a sound bath works at all.

How do I mic crystal bowls and gongs for silent disco headphones?

Use one or two condenser microphones positioned near your instruments, run them through a small mixer into the transmitter's 3.5mm or RCA input. The sound broadcasts to every headphone in real time. Setup takes about ten minutes and works with any standard mic and mixer.

Can I run a sound bath outdoors with headphones?

Yes — this is where headphones truly shine. Outdoors, delicate bowl overtones are normally lost to open air and wind within a few metres. With headphones, guests hear studio-quality sound on a beach at sunset. The 500m wireless range covers any outdoor setup.

How many headphones do I need for a sound bath event?

One per guest, since everyone listens for the entire session. Most studio sound baths run 15-30 guests, so 20-40 headphones covers regular events with spares. For festival or retreat sound journeys, 50-100 headphones lets you scale without losing intimacy.
