Frequently Asked Questions: Silent Fitness

Are the headphones sweat-proof for intense workouts?

Yes! Our headphones are built for active use with sweat-resistant materials and secure-fit design. The electronics are protected, and the padding can be wiped clean after each session. Perfect for HIIT, bootcamp, dance fitness, and spin classes.

Can instructors use a microphone channel?

Absolutely! One channel can be dedicated to instructor microphone while the other two play different workout music. Instructors can give cues, motivation, and form corrections directly through the headphones while participants groove to their chosen music style.

How do you set up silent fitness classes outdoors?

Simply place the transmitter in the centre of your workout area it has a 500m range so covers large outdoor spaces easily. Participants can spread out across parks, beaches, or rooftops without losing signal. No noise complaints, no venue restrictions.

What's the best way to set up channels for fitness classes?

We recommend: Channel 1 for instructor mic + high-energy music, Channel 2 for pure workout beats (EDM, hip-hop), and Channel 3 for lower intensity music (pop, rock). This lets participants choose their energy level while staying connected to instruction.
