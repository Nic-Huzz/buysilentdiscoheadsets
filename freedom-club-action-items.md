# Summary: Top Action Items for Headset Rental Bali

The course is designed for dropshipping ecommerce stores on Shopify, so many tactical steps don't apply directly. However, the **principles and frameworks** are gold. Here are the highest-leverage items adapted for your rental business:

## Must-Do (Transferable Principles)

1. **Branding & Trust Pages** (Days 6, 12, 13): Create/improve your About Us, Our Mission, FAQs, and Contact pages. Tell YOUR story. Make the problem real ("boring events", "noise complaints at villas") and position your headphones as the solution.

2. **Social Proof** (Days 10, 17, 30): Collect real testimonials from past events. Film events. Get UGC from happy clients. Display reviews prominently. Reply to all comments and DMs.

3. **Email Marketing** (Days 19, 20): Set up an email pop-up and welcome flow. Offer a discount/freebie for sign-up. Automate: welcome email > brand story > testimonials > urgency offer.

4. **Tiered Packages & Upsells** (Days 4, 21, 22): Create Basic / Premium / VIP packages. Offer complementary add-ons (LED wristbands, signage, extra channels). Upsell at checkout.

5. **Facebook/Instagram Ads** (Days 24-28): Create video ads from event footage. Use the hook > benefits > social proof > CTA structure. Test multiple creatives and audiences. Track metrics, optimize every 2 days.

6. **Copywriting Framework** (Day 16): Feature + Benefit always together. Bold the key phrases. Use customer language. Simple > complex.

7. **Data-Driven Optimization** (Day 28): Track CPM, CTR, CPC, conversion rate, ROAS, net profit margin. Identify "conversion bleeds" and fix them. Benchmarks: CPM <$20, CTR >1%, CPC <$2, Conv Rate >2%, Net Margin >25%.

8. **Pricing Psychology** (Day 9): End prices with 9 or 90. Show original vs discounted pricing. Volume discounts for larger bookings.

9. **Customer Support** (Day 30): Reply to every message. Turn comments into conversations. Have a clear refund/damage policy. Use support questions as product ideas.

10. **Website Speed** (Days 9, 11): Compress all images through tinypng.com. Use proper image dimensions. This alone can significantly improve conversion rates.
