## Sending Strategy

- **Daily volume:** Start at 30/day, ramp to 100/day over 2 weeks
- **Timing:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11am local time for the recipient's timezone
- **Gap between emails:** 3 days for email 1→2, 3 days for 2→3, 4 days for 3→4, 5 days for 4→5
- **Stop sending to a lead if:** they reply (any reply), they unsubscribe, email bounces
- **Personalization:** At minimum, use {{business_name}}, {{first_name}}, and {{city}}. If possible, reference something specific about their business from their website.

## Compliance

### All emails must include:
- Your real name and business name
- A physical mailing address (can use a virtual office)
- An unsubscribe mechanism ("Reply STOP to unsubscribe")
- No deceptive subject lines

### For EU recipients (GDPR):
- Legal basis: Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) — B2B outreach to publicly listed business contacts
- Add this footer to all EU-targeted emails: "You're receiving this because your business is publicly listed. Reply DELETE to have your data removed."
- Honour deletion requests within 72 hours
- Only email addresses found on public business websites (not personal emails)

### For US recipients (CAN-SPAM):
- Include physical address in email signature
- Honour opt-out requests within 10 business days

### For Australian recipients (Spam Act 2003):
- Must include clear sender identification
- Must include functional unsubscribe
- Only B2B commercial electronic messages to publicly available business addresses
