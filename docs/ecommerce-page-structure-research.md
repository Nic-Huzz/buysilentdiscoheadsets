# E-Commerce Landing Page Structure Research

**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Context:** Validating section order for B2B equipment sales page (silent disco headphones for wellness facilitators). Original page had 17 sections. Research used to restructure to 12.

## The Consensus Structure (7-9 sections)

Multiple sources converge on the same architecture. CXL, Unbounce, Gati Lab, and FoundryCRO all recommend a variation of this order:

1. **Hero** (headline + CTA + proof snippet)
2. **Social proof bar** (logos, customer count)
3. **Problem / pain** (articulate what they're dealing with)
4. **Solution / benefits** (your product as the answer)
5. **How it works** (3-step process)
6. **Deeper proof** (testimonials, case studies, results)
7. **Objection handling / guarantee**
8. **Primary CTA / pricing**
9. **FAQ**

CXL's research shows that proof visible within the first two scrollable sections increases conversions by 34%. The 9-section skeleton from Gati Lab places the primary CTA after ~7 sections of value-building. B2B pages specifically place "How it works" right after the pain/solution sections, not deep in the page. CXL's long-form study of 6,200 B2B/high-ticket pages confirms that long pages with interactive elements (calculators, collapsible FAQs) convert well for high-ticket offers, but only when strategically organized.

## Key Data Points

| Finding | Lift | Source |
|---------|------|--------|
| Social proof within first 2 scrolls | +34% conversion | CXL |
| Testimonials near CTA | +50-68% lift | Hubstaff case study, DiscoveredLabs |
| Social proof above pricing tiers | +9% conversion | Pricing page benchmark study (200+ sites) |
| Three-tier pricing vs four+ tiers | +14% | SaaS pricing benchmark |
| Single offer focus vs multiple offers | up to 266% better conversion | Involve.me / industry data |
| Long-form with interactive elements | Validated for high-ticket B2B | CXL (6,200 pages) |
| Average scroll depth | 40-60% | Industry consensus - anything past section 10-11 on a 17-section page is seen by a minority |

## How Our V2 Compared (before restructure)

The original v2 order was:

1. Hero (headline + CTA)
2. Brand logos (social proof)
3. Video grid (product in action)
4. Why Headsets (use cases)
5. Reformer analogy (positioning story)
6. "Sell More Tickets" pricing proof
7. Product specs
8. Founder story
9. How It Works (3-step process)
10. ROI Calculator
11. Packages/Pricing
12. Testimonials
13. Equipment details
14. Guarantee
15. FAQ
16. Story Timeline
17. Definition section

**What was working:**
- Hero at position 1: correct
- Brand logos at position 2: correct. Research confirms social proof within the first two scrolls lifts conversion 34%
- Video grid at position 3: strong. Visual proof early aligns with Baymard's recommendation for 3-5+ product visuals in the initial viewport area
- ROI Calculator (position 10): CXL confirms interactive elements boost high-ticket conversions

## Sections That Needed to Move

| Section | Was At | Should Be | Why |
|---------|--------|-----------|-----|
| How It Works | 9 (after founder story) | 5 (after use cases) | B2B research places this right after pain/solution. At position 9, it sits behind 4 sections of value narrative. Visitors need to understand the buying process BEFORE you build ROI arguments. |
| Testimonials | 12 (after pricing) | 6-7 (near pricing AND as a second cluster after use cases) | Testimonials placed near the CTA lift conversion 50-68%. At position 12, they're after the decision point. Split them: brief testimonials after use cases, detailed ones flanking pricing. |
| Packages/Pricing | 11 | 8-9 (after ROI proof) | Pricing should appear AFTER you've built value but not buried under 10 sections. Research shows 40-60% average scroll depth. At position 11 of 17, a significant portion of visitors never see it. Social proof placed ABOVE pricing tiers lifts conversion 9%. |
| Guarantee | 14 | Immediately after or within pricing | Guarantee is an objection-handler. Research shows it belongs adjacent to the commitment moment, not 3 sections later. |

## Sections Cut or Consolidated

- Equipment details (13), Story Timeline (16), and Definition (17) were redundant with earlier sections. Pages with a single focused offer outperform pages with multiple offers by up to 266%.
- Founder story (8) and Reformer analogy (5) both served "positioning" - merged into one narrative section.
- Cut from 17 sections to 12.

## Final Restructured Order (applied)

1. **Hero** (H1 + CTA + proof)
2. **Brand logos** (social proof within first 2 scrolls = +34% conversion)
3. **Video grid** (product in action)
4. **Why Headsets** (use cases: breathwork, dance, guided)
5. **How It Works** (3-step process - B2B consensus: before pricing)
6. **Positioning Story** (merged: Reformer analogy + Founder credibility)
7. **Testimonials** (moved near decision point = +50-68% lift)
8. **ROI Proof** ("Sell More Tickets" + ROI Calculator)
9. **Packages + Guarantee** (Hormozi value stack + tiers + guarantee adjacent to commitment)
10. **Product Specs** (for detail scrollers)
11. **FAQ**
12. **Final CTA** (purple gradient closer)
