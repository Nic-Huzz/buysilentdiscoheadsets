## Price Architecture (Hormozi framework)

### Anchor: $49/headset (branded)
- Custom logo + custom LED colours included
- Free shipping on orders over $500
- Event dashboard (6 months free)
- Full value stack included (playbooks, guides, video strategy)
- Minimum order: 10 headsets (so minimum order = $490 + $169 transmitter = $659)

### Feature Downsell: $39/headset (plain)
- No custom branding (no logo, no custom LED colours)
- No free shipping
- Still gets value stack + dashboard
- Hormozi principle: remove highest-value feature (branding = identity) so people see the loss and self-select back to $49
- Source: Money Models Course 25 - "Remove features from highest to lowest value. People will see the value in what was removed after they see the price difference."

### Continuity: Event Dashboard subscription (future)
- 6 months free with any headset order
- After trial: ~$29-49/month (TBD)
- Manages bookings, tracks gear, runs events
- Based on Scale Portal (Claude Portal) simplified for event operators
- Hormozi "Big Head, Long Tail": headset sale is the big head (cash now), dashboard is the long tail (recurring forever)
- Sunk cost + switching costs = low churn (their data, events, venue contacts all in the platform)
- Source: Mozi Money Minute "Big Head, Long Tail" + Money Models Course 26

### Future Upsell: Support + Training
- Facilitator training (breathwork, dance)
- Marketing footage pack
- Website creation
- Premium Scale Portal features
- Source: Hormozi Ep 378 "Downsell Your Upsell" - sell high upfront, then continuity feels like a downsell

### TBC: Affiliate Army

Source: $100M Leads Part 10, Ch. 8

Every facilitator who buys headsets knows other facilitators. Recruit customers as affiliates.

**Tactics to evaluate:**
- Gift card method: Give every customer a $200 gift card to hand a friend. Status transfer, not "referral." Expiration within 7-14 days forces action.
- Unlockable referral bonuses: Refer 1 friend = premium playlist pack. Refer 3 = free transmitter. Refer 5 = free marketing footage.
- Ongoing referral program via the event dashboard (track referrals inside the tool they already use)
- Hormozi math: If each customer brings 2 referrals, LTV:CAC goes from 4:1 to 12:1.

**Priority:** High. Zero cost, highest leverage. Even just the gift card method is a win.

### TBC: Seller-Based Retention (Dealer Network)

Source: Mozi Minute "Big Ticket Retention"

For physical product businesses, retention happens at the SELLER level, not the customer level. Don't need the same facilitator buying monthly. Need dealers who order monthly.

**Potential dealer types:**
- Rental companies (order 50-100 at a time, repeatedly)
- Yoga/wellness studio chains (equip every location)
- Event production companies (supply facilitators)
- Training programs (bundle gear with certification)
- Retreat centres (equip permanently)

**Metric to track:** Revenue per seller, not just per customer. 10 dealers each ordering 30 headsets quarterly = 1,200 headsets/year predictable revenue.

**Priority:** High. Transforms one-time sales into recurring physical product orders without continuity software.

### TBC: Dashboard as Expansion Revenue Engine

Source: Ep 276 + Ep 714 (purchase frequency is one of 6 ways to increase LTGP)

The event dashboard sees customer growth in real time. Use it to trigger expansion offers proactively.

**How it works:**
- Dashboard tracks: events run, headsets used per event, venue sizes, ticket revenue
- When a customer's events outgrow their headset count, the dashboard flags it
- Proactive outreach: "You ran 3 events with 30 headsets last month. Your venues hold 80. Here's your upgrade quote."
- Seasonal triggers: Festival season approaching, offer bulk restocking deals
- Replacement cycle: Batteries degrade after 2-3 years, trigger replacement offers

**Why this is genius:** You're not guessing who needs more. The data tells you. And the customer sees you as helpful, not salesy, because you're helping them grow.

**Priority:** High (builds on the dashboard that's already planned). This is the #1 reason to build the dashboard beyond continuity revenue.
