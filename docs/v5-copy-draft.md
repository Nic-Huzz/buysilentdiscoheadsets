**Q1: "What does everyone in your market believe that you think is wrong?"**

## Identity Nerves

1. **"I was sold a dream"** - validates the frustration they already feel
2. **"It's not my fault I'm not making money"** - removes shame, places it on the gap in the system
3. **"There's a system for this, and I can get it"** - hope with a specific mechanism

## Mechanism

**Two-part mechanism:**
1. **Headphones → Safety → Depth → Shift** (better sessions, from v3/v4)
2. **Playbooks → Fill Events → Price Right → Scale** (profitable sessions, new in v5)

Headphones alone make sessions better. Playbooks alone teach the business. Together they're the system the training industry never gave you.

## Emotional Tone

Empathy first. "We know. We've been there." Not anger at the industry. Compassion for the facilitator who did everything right and still can't pay rent from their passion.
