## Strategy Updates (V2 changes)

**V1 → V2 changes:**
- Lead with Starter Kit price ($1,219) not per-unit ($35) — Supers want legendary, not cheap
- Test price vs no-price versions of each ad
- "Not Another Training" validated by DAM the Demand framework — keep it
- Target specific facilitator Facebook groups (Super-Geos), not just broad interest targeting
- Zero CAC flywheel: free playlists + event guide as lead magnets alongside paid ads

**Target audience:** Wellness facilitators (breathwork coaches, yoga teachers, ecstatic dance facilitators, retreat leaders)

**Key insight:** Facilitators are NOT searching for equipment. They're searching for more training, better container skills, and deeper transformation techniques. They don't know headphones are a solution. This is category creation.

**Framework:** Category Pirates "DAM the Demand" — intercept facilitators in the "training/certification" category, redirect to equipment.

**Three ad concepts:**
1. "The Gap" — you're skilled but sessions aren't deep enough (identity hook)
2. "Not Another Training" — challenges the certification mindset (DAM the Demand)
3. "Run Sessions Anywhere" — solves the outdoor/venue problem (outcome hook)

**Testing plan:**
- Each concept x long + short x price + no-price = 12 variants
- Run at $50/day across targeted facilitator groups + 2 broad ad sets
- Kill losers after spending breakeven CPA (~$500 for Starter Kit) with zero sales
- Double down on winners

## Confidence Assessment (V2)

**High confidence (90%+):**
- Facilitators are not searching for equipment — validated by search data
- Long copy works for big-ticket wellness purchases — validated by competitor longevity
- "Not Another Training" is a valid DAM the Demand move — validated by Category Pirates
- Specific group targeting > broad interest targeting — validated by Super-Geos framework

**Medium confidence (60-80%):**
- Whether $1,219 or no-price performs better — need to test both
- Which of the 3 concepts wins — genuinely unknown until we test
- Whether FB/IG is the primary discovery channel for facilitators — Super-Geos says find the right groups, not the right platform

**Lower confidence (40-60%):**
- Exact wording of image text — will evolve through testing
- Whether paid ads or Zero CAC flywheel generates better ROI long-term — Category Pirates strongly favours flywheel

**What would increase all confidence levels:**
- Ask 5 existing buyers: "What were you trying to solve? Where did you find us? What else do you invest in?"
- Post in 3 facilitator Facebook groups (organic, not ads) and see what resonates
- Your gut as someone who talks to these people daily
