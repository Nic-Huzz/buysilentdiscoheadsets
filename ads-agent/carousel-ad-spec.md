## Design notes
- Alternate photo slides and gradient slides for visual rhythm
- Progress bar at bottom of every slide (thin white line, fills left to right)
- Subtle swipe arrow on right edge (except last slide)
- All text must be readable - use dark overlays on photo slides
- Keep text minimal per slide - one idea each
- Feature pills on slide 3 should be horizontal/wrapped, not a 2x2 grid
