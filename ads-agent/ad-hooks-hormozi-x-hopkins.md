## Weakest Link Across All Hooks

**Immediate Action (Rule 10) fails on nearly every hook.** None have a real time-bound reason to act now. Options:
- "This month: 5 free headsets with orders above 10" (already on the landing page)
- "Pricing locked at $39 through [month]"  
- "Next batch ships [date]" (real shipping schedule)
- Don't force it. Hopkins says: "Not fake urgency - real service urgency." If there's no genuine deadline, a strong service CTA ("message me") is better than a manufactured one.

## How to Use These

1. **Pick 5-10 hooks** to test as primary text on your carousel ads
2. **Each hook = one ad variation.** Same carousel, different caption. This is Hormozi's volume play.
3. **The carousel handles the visual story.** The hook handles the scroll-stop.
4. **Track which hooks drive leads in Supabase**, not just clicks in Meta.
5. **Kill losers after 7 days.** Scale winners.
