## Hormozi - Key Principles for Paid Ads

### Creative Volume is THE Strategy
> "Record 10 videos, then create 30 hooks for each. You now have 300 ads, test them all, see which is converting best and double down at it."

> "Top 1% of FB advertising vs remaining 99%, they split test 11 times more content. This is why they're able to scale 10x & 100x more."

> "You're going to lose money when you create ads. You have to spend money before you make money. And most your ads aren't going to see massive returns. But the one's that do, will make it all back."
> - "Get used to losing a lot, to win big."

### 80% Effort Into Hooks
> "80% of your effort needs to go into the first 5 seconds. First impression to hook them is everything."

**4 Hook Formulas:**
1. **Labels:** "Are you a... (homeowner?)"
2. **Yes-Questions:** "Do you sweat at night? Toss and turn?"
3. **If-Then:** "If you're born between 1984 and 1975 then you may qualify for..."
4. **Ridiculous Results:** "Gym opens and overfills first day..."

### Ad Creation Matrix (What x Who x When)
- **What** = 8 elements from the Value Equation (dream outcome, likelihood, time delay, effort - each positive + negative)
- **Who** = describe benefit from every perspective (wife, kids, boss, friends, colleagues)
- **When** = past, present, future (positive + negative)
> "Motivation comes from deprivation. We don't know where they're lacking status, so we play all the cards."
> "You can't hit all of them in one ad, but gives you a variety to endlessly choose from."

### Make Ads Look Like Content
> "Dont want it to look like an ad. Want it to look like content."
> "Best ads are content that worked."

### Study Before Creating
> "Look at ads library. Find top ads in your sector running the longest with best engagement. Transcribe 50 ads. Look how they hook, retain, reward + make an offer."

### Ad Structure
> "Call out (hook): get their attention"
> "Value (retain): build trust so they're more willing to take the next step"
> "CTA (reward): to take the next step"

### Platform Isn't the Problem
> "Platform hasn't 'capped' your ad. They want to take your money. What has 'capped' you is how good your ad is. It converts in a small pond, but isn't good enough to convert in a large."

### Audience: Puddle to Ocean
> "Rather be too specific to too small of an audience."
> "Start in the puddle, then pond, then lake, then ocean."
> "Having the highest ROI in the puddle then affords you the money to be a little less efficient in the pond and so on."

### Budget Mentality
> "If you do advertising right, when you put a dollar in, you get a dollar + more back. Having an 'advertising budget' is foolish and shows you're not doing ads right."

> "Paid ads = learning budget"

### Unit Economics
> "LTGP / CAC > 3 = home run, something you can scale to the moon"
> "Alex targets 10:1, has achieved 36:1"

### Upsell for Cash Flow
> "Create a $100 upsell that 20% of people take. Cash flow positive from day 1."
> "Rather than wait 3 months or take outside money, can do it straight away."

### The Core Four (Paid Ads is #3)
1. Warm outreach: 100 people/day
2. Content: 100 minutes/day
3. Paid ads: $100/day
4. Cold outreach: 100 people/day

### Lead Magnet
> "Complete solution to a narrow problem + it should reveal the next problem which your core offer solves."

## Freedom Club - Key Principles for Paid Ads

### Campaign Structure (Day 27)
- 5 ad sets at $10/day each ($50/day total)
- Each ad set targets a different interest
- 2 video ads per ad set (C1 + C2)
- Use same Post ID across all ad sets to stack engagement
- Schedule for midnight for full day of data

### Social Proof Stacking
- Use "Existing Post" when duplicating ad sets
- All ad sets show the same creative with the same Post ID
- Comments and reactions stack across ad sets
- One ad with 50 comments outperforms 5 ads with 10 each

### Optimization Cadence (Day 28)
- Check every 2 days
- Kill ad set if spent > breakeven CPA with zero sales
- Never touch what is working
- Horizontal scaling: duplicate winners with related interests

### Benchmarks
- CPM: <$20
- CTR: >1%
- CPC: <$2
- Conversion rate: >2%
- Hook rate: >30% (3-second video plays / impressions)
- Average video play time: >3 seconds
- Net margin: >25%

### Creative Creation (Day 24)
- Create 2 video ads (C1 and C2)
- Square format (1:1) for social media
- Always add subtitles
- Structure: hook > benefits > social proof > CTA
- "Do NOT add a logo watermark to the ad - make it look like organic content, not an ad"
- Background music at 8% volume
- Keep ads 15-30 seconds

### Kill or Scale (Day 28-29)
- After 2 days: determine bucket (no sales / unprofitable with sales / profitable)
- "Winners are born winners - if an ad set is failing after spending breakeven CPA, it won't magically improve"
- Scale by 20% every 3 days on winning ad sets
- Check attribution delays before killing (sales may appear later)

### Ad Copy Formula (Day 25)
- Bold benefit claim
- 3 bullet points (key benefits)
- Irresistible offer
- Shortened URL

2. **Write 10-15 hooks** using Hormozi's 4 formulas (Labels, Yes-Questions, If-Then, Ridiculous Results) applied to our product.
3. **Film raw video content** at next event. Both frameworks say video wins over static.
4. **Launch with a mix**: carousel (already built) + raw content-style ads, multiple hooks, stacked Post IDs where possible.

### Hormozi's Ad Creation Matrix Applied to Us

**What (8 elements from Value Equation):**
- Dream outcome (+): charge 2x per session, 150+ attendees, premium brand
- Dream outcome (-): stuck at $30/class, small groups, commodity facilitator
- Likelihood (+): 300+ events prove it works, named clients
- Likelihood (-): "is it just a novelty?"
- Time delay (+): paid off before second event
- Time delay (-): "another $3,000 training that takes 6 months"
- Effort (+): plug in and go, zero setup
- Effort (-): venue acoustics, speaker permits, noise complaints

**Who (perspectives):**
- The facilitator herself
- Her participants ("they couldn't stop talking about it")
- Other facilitators ("all the leaders use them now")
- Her business partner / accountant (ROI math)

**When (timeframes):**
- Past: "I went from 20 to 150+"
- Present: "9D, Dawnbreaker, Sanctum all charge 2-3x more right now"
- Future: "Facilitators who adopt now will run the premium tier"

This matrix generates dozens of unique ad angles from combinations. Each ad picks one What x Who x When combination.
