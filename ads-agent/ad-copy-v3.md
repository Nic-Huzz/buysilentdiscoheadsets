## Ad 5: "The Objection Killer"

Hopkins rationale: Ch.18 (Positive Framing) + Ch.7 (Specificity) + Ch.3 (Service). Opens with the reader's exact objection, then answers with competitors' real pricing, not our claims. Every number is verifiable. The reader trusts it because we're citing others, not ourselves.

Source validation: Email sequence day 5 ("Do headphones actually make a difference?") confirms this is the #1 real objection from prospects.

## Testing Plan (V3)

**Phase 1: Relaunch (Week 1-2)**
- Campaign: Traffic objective
- Budget: $15-20/day, 7+ continuous days
- Ad set: Breathwork audience
- Creative: Reformer carousel (updated)
- Caption: "The Gap" long version (no price)
- UTM: utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=breathwork&utm_content=carousel-gap

**Phase 2: Caption Test (Week 3-4)**
- Same everything, swap caption only:
  - A: "The Gap" (control)
  - B: "The Math" long version (no price)
  - C: "The Objection Killer" long version (no price)
- Kill losers after 500 impressions each with no clicks, or after 7 days

**Phase 3: Audience Test (Week 5+)**
- Take the winning caption from Phase 2
- Run across all 5 audiences with identical creative
- This is the clean audience test that campaign 1 couldn't do (confounded by creative)

**Phase 4: Retargeting**
- Custom audience: website visitors last 180 days
- Exclude: Lead pixel event converters
- Budget: $5-10/day
- Creative: Whichever caption DIDN'T win in Phase 2 (second touch = different angle)

## Hard Rules (carry forward from V2)
- Never fabricate specifics. If a claim needs verification, flag it as [VERIFY].
- Never use em dashes. Use periods, commas, or " - " instead.
- Headphones are on-ear design. Never say "over-ear."
- Do not invent prices or offers that aren't in the original ad.
- Price: $39/headphone, $169/transmitter. Never copy prices from existing pages.
