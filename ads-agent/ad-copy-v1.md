## Strategy

**Target audience:** Wellness facilitators (breathwork coaches, yoga teachers, ecstatic dance facilitators, retreat leaders)

**Key insight from research:** Facilitators are NOT searching for equipment. They're searching for:
1. More certifications/training (thinking skills = better sessions)
2. How to create safety/container
3. Better playlists/music
4. How to make their sessions more immersive

They don't know headphones are a solution. This is category creation, not product comparison.

**Framework:** Category Pirates "DAM the Demand" - intercept facilitators where they're already thinking, redirect to a solution they didn't know existed.

**Three ad concepts based on facilitator mindset:**
1. "The Gap" - you're skilled but sessions aren't deep enough
2. "Not Another Training" - challenges the certification mindset
3. "Run Sessions Anywhere" - solves the outdoor/venue acoustics problem

**Testing plan:** Each concept in long + short copy = 6 ad variants. Run at $50/day across 5 ad sets. Kill losers after spending breakeven CPA (~$500 for Starter Kit) with zero sales. Double down on winners.

## Confidence Assessment

**High confidence:**
- Facilitators are not searching for equipment (validated by search data)
- Long copy works for big-ticket wellness purchases (validated by competitor ad longevity)
- The "safety/container" angle matches the #1 facilitator concern

**Medium confidence:**
- "Not Another Training" hook - provocative, could resonate or alienate
- Specific price points on images ($1,219, $35) - may need testing vs no-price versions
- Whether Facebook/Instagram is where facilitators discover equipment

**Low confidence:**
- Exact wording of image text - will need A/B testing
- Which of the 3 concepts will win - genuinely don't know until we test

**What would increase confidence:**
- Poll your existing buyers: "What were you trying to solve when you bought?"
- Test in a Facebook group before spending ad money
- Your gut reaction as someone who talks to these facilitators daily
