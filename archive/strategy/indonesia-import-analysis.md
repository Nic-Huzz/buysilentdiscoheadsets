## 3. Required Licenses & Certifications

### NIB (Business Identification Number) — ✅ OBTAINED

- ✅ **Status: Already have NIB**
- NIB must include import activity in its KBLI codes — verify this is the case
- If KBLI codes don't cover import activity, an amendment via OSS may be needed

### SDPPI Certification (Sertifikat Alat dan Perangkat Telekomunikasi)

- **Mandatory** for RF wireless devices — silent disco headsets qualify
- Regulation: Permenkominfo No. 3/2024, Keputusan Menteri No. 469/2025
- Must be in place **before customs clearance**
- Valid for 3 years
- Timeline: 2–4 weeks (document evaluation) or 4–8 weeks (lab testing)
- If headsets have FCC/CE certification, document evaluation (faster) may be possible

#### Documents needed from Chinese supplier:
1. RF test reports (from accredited lab)
2. Technical specifications (frequency, power, modulation)
3. CE/FCC certificates and full test reports
4. Product photos showing brand and model number
5. User manual
6. Declaration of Conformity

### ACFTA Form E (Certificate of Origin)

- Not mandatory, but saves ~$264 in duty (reduces from 5% to 0%)
- Issued by the Chinese manufacturer/exporter
- Ask supplier: "Can you provide a Form E (ACFTA Certificate of Origin)?"

## 5. Shipping: Jakarta vs Bali

| Factor | Jakarta | Bali |
|--------|---------|------|
| Freight cost | Cheaper (major hub) | 15–30% more |
| Route availability | Direct from all Chinese cities | Fewer routes, often transits Jakarta |
| Customs clearance | Faster, larger facilities | Smaller office |
| SDPPI office | Headquartered in Jakarta | Not local |

**Recommendation:** Ship to Jakarta, then domestic transfer to Bali. Often cheaper than direct-to-Bali even with the extra leg.

If using DDP, ask supplier if price differs between Jakarta and Bali delivery.

## 7. What's Still Required

### ✅ Done
- **NIB** — obtained

### 🔲 Still needed before import
1. **SDPPI certification** — mandatory for RF wireless headsets, must be in place before customs clearance (2–8 weeks lead time). This is the critical path item.
2. **NPWP (tax ID)** — confirm this is active and linked to NIB
3. **API-U not held** — income tax rate is 7.5% (not 2.5%). Consider applying for API-U via OSS if planning future imports to reduce this cost

### 🔲 Action items
1. **Ask supplier for Form E** (ACFTA Certificate of Origin) — saves ~$264
2. **Ask supplier for FCC/CE test reports** — speeds up SDPPI certification
3. **Clarify DDP scope** — does it include SDPPI certification and door delivery to Bali?
4. **If DAP:** Contact Mouse & Bear or Emerhub for customs brokerage + SDPPI handling
5. **Start SDPPI certification early** — it's the longest lead item (2–8 weeks)
6. **Confirm HS code with supplier** — verify 8518.30.10.00 is correct for the specific models
7. **Verify NIB KBLI codes** — confirm import activity is listed in your NIB's registered business classifications
