### Step 4: TAM Calculation

```
TAM = (Price per unit) × (Units per consumer) × (Number of consumers)
```

**Conservative estimate:**
- Price per unit: $39/headset
- Units per consumer: Average order ~20 headsets + 1 transmitter = $949
- Number of consumers: ~500,000 wellness facilitators globally (yoga, breathwork, meditation, ecstatic dance, sound healing)
- **Even at 1% penetration: 5,000 buyers × $949 = $4.7M**

**How Supers expand it:**

| Variable | How Our Supers Expand It |
|----------|------------------------|
| **Price per unit** | Supers want larger sets (50-100+ headsets for retreats/festivals). Package prices increase. |
| **Units per consumer** | Supers buy headsets, transmitters, charging stations, replacements. They outfit multiple rooms or locations. |
| **Number of consumers** | A breathwork facilitator who buys tells their yoga teacher friend. Facilitators cluster in communities. One sale at a retreat centre leads to every visiting facilitator seeing the equipment. |

## Framework 3: The Marketing Flywheel (Operating Model)

### Current State: Still Funnel-ish

Right now the motion is mostly: Meta ads → landing page → quote form → manual follow-up. That's a funnel. Need to convert to flywheel.

### The 3-Phase Flywheel for Headsets

#### Phase 1: Attract Supers with Unique POV

**Our POV:** "Headsets are the Reformer of wellness. Facilitators who adopt them now will run the premium-tier classes of the future."

This POV:
- Frames a different problem (it's not about parties, it's about professional equipment)
- Creates urgency (early adopters win the premium positioning)
- Speaks facilitator-to-facilitator (Nic IS a facilitator)

**Where to broadcast:** Instagram (facilitator community), Meta ads targeting facilitator job titles, cold email sequences, AI citation optimization (already 40.5% share)

#### Phase 2: Equip Supers to Make More Supers

**The natural WOM loop:**
- Facilitator buys headsets → runs a session → participants experience the "sound cocoon"
- Participants say "Where did you get these?" or "I want these for MY classes"
- Every session is a live demo. Every participant is a potential buyer.

**How to accelerate:**
- Send buyers a "share kit" - shareable content/templates they can post about their headset sessions
- Feature buyer events on the site and socials (already doing brand videos)
- Create a facilitator community (Slack/WhatsApp) where buyers share tips, playlists, session formats

#### Phase 3: Delight Supers

- Personalized quote pages (already doing this - alice.html, chris.html, etc.)
- 3 free headsets with any order (current promo)
- Fast, human WhatsApp support from Nic directly
- Playlist recommendations, session format guides, "how to charge premium" content

### The Flywheel Motion

```
Facilitator buys headsets
    → Runs sessions with them (live demo)
    → Participants experience it (WOM)
    → Other facilitators hear about it
    → They visit the site / get a quote
    → They buy (more data, more testimonials)
    → Repeat
```

### Flywheel Team

| Role | Who |
|------|-----|
| **AI Co-Founder** | Claude (content, ads, copy, strategy, lead capture automation) |
| **Native Analog** | Nic - the facilitator who lives the problem, runs events, knows the market |

### Zero CAC Flywheel Vision

Every headset session IS marketing. The product markets itself in use. The vision: get enough facilitators running headset sessions that word-of-mouth replaces paid ads entirely.

**Current friction:** Most facilitators don't know headsets exist as a tool. Once they see one session, they're sold. The bottleneck is the first experience, not the conversion.

We're not coronating a king yet. We need revenue momentum. The best strike type for us is **Functional** - prove the product works NOW.

**Strike 1: "The 30-Day Headset Challenge" (Low Budget)**

- **Outcome:** Creates content for months. Positions Nic as the category leader. Every participating facilitator becomes an ambassador.
