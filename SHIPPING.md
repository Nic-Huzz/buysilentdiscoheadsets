# Shipping Bible

Source-of-truth for shipping costs. DDP = Delivered Duty Paid (all customs clearance and duties included, door to door). Transit time: 8-10 days after takeoff from factory.

## Box Weights (headsets only)

| Quantity | Dimensions | Weight |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| 10 headsets | 30 x 30 x 30 cm | 5 kg |
| 20 headsets | 48 x 23 x 34 cm | 7.5 kg |
| 30 headsets | 44 x 37 x 27 cm | 10 kg |

**Add ~0.5 kg for transmitter + charging cable.**

## Quick Shipping Calculator

1. Look up box weight from quantity above
2. Add 0.5 kg for transmitter + cable
3. Look up total weight in the destination rate table below
4. For weights between rows, round UP to next 0.5 kg step

## Notes

- These rates are from the factory's shipping partner, NOT DHL Express
- DDP means buyer pays nothing extra on delivery - no surprise customs fees
- Rates are for air freight, not sea (sea is cheaper but 30-45 days)
- For destinations not listed here, ask the factory for a quote
- Transmitter box dimensions: small, fits inside headset box
- As more destination rate cards come in, add them as new sections here
