## Campaign Structure (from Freedom Club Day 27)

**Budget:** $50/day total
**Structure:** 1 Campaign > 5 Ad Sets > 2 Ads each

| Ad Set | Targeting | Budget |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Interest: Event Planning | $10/day |
| 2 | Interest: Breathwork / Yoga Retreat | $10/day |
| 3 | Interest: Wedding Planning | $10/day |
| 4 | Interest: DJ Equipment / Music Production | $10/day |
| 5 | Broad (no interests, 22-65+) | $10/day |

**Each ad set gets:** Ad 1 (Silence Reveal) + Ad 2 (Buy vs Rent)
**Use the same Post ID** across all ad sets so engagement stacks.
**Schedule:** Start at midnight for a full day of data.
**Optimization:** Review every 2 days. Kill ad sets that spend past breakeven cost-per-purchase with no sales.

**Breakeven ROAS by package:**
- Starter Kit ($463): Any sale from <$463 ad spend = profitable
- Professional ($1,149): Any sale from <$1,149 ad spend = profitable
- Enterprise ($2,119): Any sale from <$2,119 ad spend = profitable

These are high-ticket items — you don't need many sales. Even 1 Enterprise sale per week at $50/day ad spend = $1,769 profit/week.
